There are things we notice only when we give them time. A familiar street, a room we pass through every day, a conversation we think we have heard before: each can reveal something new when we stop trying to get to the next thing.

## Looking more closely

The first task is to stay a little longer. Watch how the light moves across a wall. Listen to the pause before an answer. Ask a second question, then leave enough room for the reply.

> Attention begins with the possibility that there is more to see.

Writing gives these small observations somewhere to live. It lets us return to a moment and discover what we missed, without pretending that every detail needs to become a lesson.

Sometimes a page is simply a record: this happened, someone was here, and for a little while we paid attention.
