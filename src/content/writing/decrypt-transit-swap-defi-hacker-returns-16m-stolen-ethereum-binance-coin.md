A hacker this morning [returned](https://twitter.com/TransitFinance/status/1576463550557483008) 70% of the $23 million that was [stolen](https://slowmist.medium.com/cross-chain-dex-aggregator-transit-swap-hacked-analysis-74ba39c22020) from the decentralized exchange ([DEX](https://decrypt.co/resources/what-is-decentralized-exchange-dex)) Transit Swap on Saturday.

The cross-chain DEX yesterday [revealed](https://twitter.com/TransitFinance) that the hacker took advantage of a bug in its code, which the company identified in a self-review.

"After a self-review by the Transit Finance team, it was confirmed that the incident was caused by a hacker attack due to a bug in the code. We are deeply sorry," [tweeted](https://twitter.com/TransitFinance/status/1576411330453479424/photo/1) Transit Swap.

Transit Finance is the name of the larger crypto project, of which Transit Swap is one product. The project also has an [NFT](https://decrypt.co/resources/non-fungible-tokens-nfts-explained-guide-learn-blockchain) marketplace.

It also appears that the hacker left behind a considerable trail, with Transit Swap [saying](https://twitter.com/TransitFinance) it had identified the hacker’s IP, email address, and associated on-chain addresses with the help of crypto security companies SlowMist, Bitrace, PeckShield, TokenPocket, and TransitFinance.

The hacker had reportedly transferred 2,500 [Binance Coin](https://decrypt.co/price/binance-coin) (BNB), or $710,000 at today’s prices, to Tornado Cash, the beleaguered cryptocurrency “tumbler” at the center of a [legal battle](https://decrypt.co/109267/coinbase-backs-tornado-cash-lawsuit-against-u-s-treasury) between crypto investors, [Coinbase](https://decrypt.co/46690/coinbase-review-a-great-jumping-on-point-for-bitcoin-beginners) and the U.S. Treasury.

The Transit Swap team [said](https://slowmist.medium.com/cross-chain-dex-aggregator-transit-swap-hacked-analysis-74ba39c22020) in a Medium post announcing the attack that the hacker had been depositing and withdrawing funds from crypto exchange LATOKEN, among other platforms.

Transit Swap earlier [tweeted](https://twitter.com/TransitFinance/status/1576603343949996032) it was working with users to formulate a return plan for the stolen funds.

## Crypto hacks on the rise

The hack followed the massive $160 million [stolen](https://decrypt.co/110131/algorithmic-market-maker-wintermute-hacked-for-160m) from algorithmic market maker Wintermute two weeks ago, with crypto analysis firm Chainalysis [reporting](https://decrypt.co/107613/crypto-crime-falls-15-along-with-bear-market-chainalysis) $1.9 billion worth of hacks for the period ending in July, an increase of almost 60% over last year’s numbers.

But the crown for this year’s most high-profile hack, so far, goes to the $622 million [taken](https://decrypt.co/96322/hacker-622-million-axie-infinity-ronin-ethereum) from blockchain game Axie Infinity in March, which saw more than 173,000 WETH siphoned off.

Still, crypto crime in the form of scams was down 15% for the year, according to Chainalysis, compared to the 36% decrease in legitimate transactions following the crypto crash earlier this year.

The firm said that a combination of declining token prices and the lack of new, unsuspecting crypto users might have made scams less enticing to carry out.
